# Rook support for passed pawns

- Time: `2026-08-09T21:44:51+01:00`
- Branch: `agent/eval-rook-passer`
- Base: `origin/main` at `bec7218`
- Result: kept without a strength claim

## Problem

The evaluator rewarded passed-pawn advancement and general rook activity, but it did not recognize the classical endgame arrangement of an active rook behind its own passed pawn. A rook with a clear file behind the pawn supports each advance while retaining lateral activity.

## Change

- While visiting each already-identified passed pawn, intersect its occupancy-aware rook ray with own rooks.
- Award `+8` middlegame and `+18` endgame only when the rook is on a lower relative rank than its pawn.
- The sliding ray rejects any intervening piece. The own-rook filter rejects an opposing blockader; the relative-rank test rejects rooks in front or beside the pawn.
- Leave passed-pawn detection, immediate blockage, rook file bonuses, attack maps and move generation unchanged.

## Focused regressions

Paired positions hold material, pawn terms, rook activity, king safety and phase constant while moving the rook between a supporting and control square.

- The white supporting position scores `741`; its control scores `724`.
- Exact color/rank mirrors with Black to move return the same scores.
- The observed 17 cp delta is the tapered result of the raw `+8/+18` term in the fixture's rook endgame phase.
- Mirrored negative fixtures cover an own rook in front, an intervening piece and an enemy rook behind the passer.

## Rejected screens before the kept change

Two search experiments were built, tested, benchmarked and then removed before this evaluator change:

- Carrying a root fail-high score into the aspiration retry increased the 200-position depth-10 total from 53,009,557 to 54,043,766 nodes (`+1.951%`).
- Suppressing ProbCut after a sufficiently deep TT upper/exact refutation reduced the same total by only 764 nodes (`-0.0014%`). A three-position depth-13 check saved only 932 of roughly 5.45 million nodes.

Neither experiment remains in the source tree and neither is claimed as progress.
