## Problem

Captures-only quiescence could stop one move before a quiet checkmate and treat the position as a static material win. In the target position, a poisoned queen move looked winning at depth one even though Black had `Qc2#` immediately.

Target FEN:

```text
3r2k1/ppB2pp1/6b1/7p/1n1Pq3/2Q3P1/PP3P1P/2KR3R w - - 1 22
```

## Failing baseline

- Restricted `go depth 1 searchmoves c3b4`: `+617`, 2 nodes, PV `c3b4`.
- Restricted depth 2: mate against Proton, 102 nodes, PV `c3b4 e4c2`.
- Stockfish independently confirms `e4c2#` after `c3b4`.

## Change

- Added a bounded scan for immediate legal quiet mates at quiescence nodes within the first three plies.
- The scan uses the existing per-ply move buffers and nested make/unmake checks; it does not recurse through general quiet checks.
- A found mate is stored with the exact ply-adjusted score and the mating move is retained in the principal variation.
- Added a native regression requiring depth one to report mate and PV `c3b4 e4c2`.
