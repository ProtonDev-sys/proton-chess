# Preserve checking captures in quiescence

- Time: `2026-08-10T00:02:35+01:00`
- Branch: `agent/qsearch-checking-captures`
- Base: `origin/main` at `504100b`
- Result: kept as a correctness repair; no strength claim

## Problem

Quiescence applied delta pruning before making a capture, so it did not yet know whether the move gave check. The later negative-SEE pruning correctly exempted checking captures, but a move could already have been discarded by the earlier delta test.

On this position:

```text
5bkb/7p/8/7Q/8/3B4/8/6K1 w - - 0 1
```

both `Qxh7#` and `Bxh7#` capture a pawn and give mate. At absolute ply 4, a qsearch window whose alpha equals `stand_pat + pawn + 180` made the old code delta-prune both moves and return stand-pat. The candidate searches the checking capture and returns the exact mate score `32000 - 5 = 31995` with the mating move in its PV.

## Change

- Added `Position::gives_check`, which reconstructs occupancy and the moving side's attacker sets after a structurally valid pseudo-move without changing board state.
- Ordinary captures clear and then reoccupy the destination. En passant separately clears the captured pawn. Promotions replace the pawn with the promoted type. Castling relocates both king and rook.
- Pawn, knight, king, diagonal-slider and orthogonal-slider attacks are tested from the enemy king using the hypothetical post-move position.
- The predicate is geometric. It does not decide whether the moving side leaves its own king in check; `make_move` remains authoritative for legality.
- Qsearch now combines its existing delta and negative-SEE conditions. A pruning candidate is skipped before `make_move` only when the exact predicate says it does not give check.
- Promotions and check evasions retain their existing exemptions.

## Regressions

- The mate fixture derives its pruning boundary from the live stand-pat instead of pinning an unrelated evaluator constant. It requires the exact mate distance, one-move mating PV, entry into the tactical line and full position restoration.
- A second direct qsearch fixture proves that a delta-prunable en-passant discovered check is searched and restores FEN/key state.
- Prediction is compared with `make_move` followed by `in_check(new side)` for every legal generated move in curated positions covering:
  - direct checking captures;
  - ordinary rook and bishop discoveries;
  - white and black en-passant discoveries;
  - an en-passant destination that reblocks a bishop ray;
  - a king capture that continues to block a rook ray;
  - checking promotions and castling;
  - Kiwipete.
- The same differential oracle runs over 12 deterministic 16-ply playouts, checking 192 additional positions.

## Review

They confirmed pawn direction, queens in both slider sets, ordinary destination reoccupation, source-vacation discoveries, en-passant removal/reblocking, promotions, castling and king moves.

Review requests for an explicit en-passant qsearch regression, ordinary discovered-check fixtures, destination-reblocking fixtures and an API legality-contract comment were implemented. A suggested micro-optimization to short-circuit `captured_value` when SEE is already negative was not applied after freezing the played binary; it remains unclaimed and can be screened separately.
